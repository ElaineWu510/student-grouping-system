04 學習動機量表 Learning Motivation Scale (MSLQ)

請根據您對本課程的期待，選擇最符合您的選項
1=非常不同意, 2=不同意, 3=普通, 4=同意, 5=非常同意

內在動機 Intrinsic Motivation

11. 在這樣的課堂中，我喜歡具有挑戰性的課程內容，這樣我才能學習。

In a class like this, I prefer course material that really challenges me so I can learn.

12. 在這樣的課堂中，我喜歡能引起我好奇心的課程內容，即使它很難學習。

In a class like this, I prefer course material that arouses my curiosity, even if it is difficult to learn.

13. 當我有機會時，我會選擇可以學到東西的課程作業，即使它們不能保證得到好成績。

When I have the opportunity, I choose course assignments that I can learn from even if they don't guarantee a good grade.

外在動機 Extrinsic Motivation

14. 在這門課程中獲得好成績對我來說是最令人滿意的事情。

Getting a good grade in this class is the most satisfying thing to me.

15. 現在對我最重要的是提高我的平均成績以成為更好的學生。

The most important thing for me right now is improving my grade point average to become a better student.

16. 我想在這門課程中表現好，因為向家人、朋友、雇主或其他人展示我的能力很重要。

I want to do well in this class because it is important to show my ability to my family, friends, employer, or others.

05 自我效能量表 Self-Efficacy Scale

請根據您對自己能力的信心程度，選擇最符合的選項
1=非常不同意, 2=不同意, 3=沒意見, 4=同意, 5=非常同意

17. 只要我夠努力，我總是能設法解決困難的問題。

I can always manage to solve difficult problems if I try hard enough.

18. 如果有人反對我，我能找到方法達成我想要的。

If someone opposes me, I can find the means and ways to get what I want.

19. 對我來說，堅持目標並完成它是容易的。

It is easy for me to stick to my aims and accomplish my goals.

20. 我有信心能有效處理突發事件。

I am confident that I could deal efficiently with unexpected events.

21. 因為我的應變能力，我知道如何處理意料之外的情況。

Thanks to my resourcefulness, I know how to handle unforeseen situations.

22. 只要投入必要的努力，我能解決大部分的問題。

I can solve most problems if I invest the necessary effort.

23. 面對困難時我能保持冷靜，因為我可以依靠自己的應對能力。

I can remain calm when facing difficulties because I can rely on my coping abilities.

24. 當我遇到問題時，我通常能找到幾個解決方案。

When I am confronted with a problem, I can usually find several solutions.

25. 如果我遇到麻煩，我通常能想出解決方法。

If I am in trouble, I can usually think of a solution.

26. 我通常能處理任何發生在我身上的事。

I can usually handle whatever comes my way.
